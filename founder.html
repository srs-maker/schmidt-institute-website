Why the Schmidt Institute Exists

The Schmidt Institute was created to help educators and organizations design learning experiences that are more responsive, practical, data-informed, and student-centered.

Educators Need Earlier Insight

Instructors often begin teaching before they fully understand student readiness, confidence, goals, prior experience, and learning preferences.

Data Should Lead to Action

Assessment data should not sit in a report. It should help educators plan instruction, identify support needs, and improve learning experiences.

Innovation Should Be Practical

AI, analytics, and educational technology should support meaningful teaching decisions, not create more complexity for educators.

Why I Created CohortView™

CohortView™ was created from a simple instructional problem: instructors often begin teaching before they fully understand who their students are.

Students Bring More Than Grades

Students enter courses with different levels of confidence, experience, goals, learning preferences, and support needs.

Faculty Need Earlier Insight

Traditional course systems often show performance after students have already struggled. CohortView™ is designed to inform instruction earlier.

Information Should Improve Teaching

The goal is not just to collect information. The goal is to help educators use student data to make better instructional decisions.

Teaching Philosophy

Instruction should be responsive, practical, inclusive, and connected to student goals. The best educational tools help instructors make better decisions while helping students feel seen, supported, and prepared.

Student-Centered

Instruction should begin with an understanding of students’ strengths, needs, lived experience, goals, and learning context.

Data-Informed

Assessment data should help instructors plan instruction, identify gaps, personalize support, and improve student success.

Workforce-Aligned

Learning should connect academic skills with real-world applications, career pathways, and workplace readiness.

Innovation-Focused

Technology, AI, and instructional design should be used thoughtfully to improve learning, not replace meaningful teaching.
