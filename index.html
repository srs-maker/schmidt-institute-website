Understanding Students Before Instruction Begins

LearningLens transforms formative assessment data into actionable instructional insights. Educators gain a deeper understanding of student readiness, learning preferences, educational goals, career aspirations, and content knowledge before instruction starts.

How It Works

1. Assess

Students complete a formative assessment.

2. Analyze

LearningLens generates readiness and learning profiles.

3. Act

Faculty receive KPI dashboards and intervention recommendations.
