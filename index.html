Introducing CohortView™

See your students before you teach.

CohortView™ is being designed as an instructional intelligence platform that transforms formative assessment data into actionable teaching insights before instruction begins.

  • Student readiness profiles
  • Learning preference analysis
  • Educational and career goal mapping
  • Course-specific knowledge diagnostics
  • Instructor KPI dashboards
  • Instructional recommendations
