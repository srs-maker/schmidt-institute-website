Courses

Professional learning experiences for educators, instructional designers, faculty leaders, and organizations interested in instructional innovation, student readiness, AI integration, and data-informed teaching.

Courses Coming Soon

The Schmidt Institute is developing practical, educator-focused courses designed to help instructors turn student data, instructional technology, and emerging tools into meaningful teaching strategies.

Using CohortView™

Learn how to interpret student readiness profiles, cohort-level analytics, KPI dashboards, and instructional recommendations.

Data-Informed Teaching

Build confidence using assessment results, student feedback, learning analytics, and classroom data to improve instructional decisions.

AI for Educators

Explore practical and ethical uses of artificial intelligence for course design, feedback, accessibility, student support, and instructional planning.

Competency-Based Education Design

Design learning experiences centered on mastery, outcomes, formative assessment, evidence of learning, and flexible student progress.

Instructional Analytics

Learn how to identify patterns in student learning, interpret KPI data, and connect analytics to meaningful course improvements.

Student Success Strategies

Use early student insight to design interventions, build confidence, support engagement, and create more responsive learning environments.

Designed for Working Educators

Courses will be built for busy instructors and education professionals who need practical strategies they can apply immediately.

Flexible Format

Courses are planned as self-paced learning experiences with videos, downloadable materials, examples, and applied exercises.

Applied Activities

Each course will include reflection prompts, templates, planning tools, and practice activities connected to real teaching contexts.

Professional Development

Future courses may include certificates of completion, cohort-based workshops, and institutional training options.

Potential Learning Pathways

Courses may eventually be organized into certificate pathways aligned with instructional innovation and data-informed teaching.

CohortView™ Certification

A pathway for educators using CohortView™ to interpret student readiness data and apply instructional recommendations.

Instructional Intelligence Certificate

A professional development pathway focused on using student insight, formative assessment, and learning analytics to improve teaching.

AI-Enhanced Teaching Certificate

A future pathway for educators integrating artificial intelligence into planning, instruction, feedback, and student support.
