CohortView™

See your students before you teach.

CohortView transforms formative assessment data into actionable instructional intelligence before instruction begins. Understand student readiness, learning preferences, educational goals, career aspirations, and content knowledge gaps before the first lesson is taught.

The Problem

Instructors often begin teaching with limited insight into who their students are, what they already know, how they prefer to learn, and where they may need support.

Student needs are discovered too late

Many learning gaps are identified only after students miss assignments, score poorly, or disengage.

Course planning is often reactive

Faculty may spend the first few weeks trying to determine what students need instead of acting on early data.

Traditional systems look backward

Most LMS data shows what happened after instruction begins. CohortView is designed to inform teaching before instruction starts.

The CohortView™ Solution

CohortView transforms formative assessment data into instructional intelligence that helps educators plan, personalize, and intervene earlier.

Students complete a formative assessment

The assessment gathers information about readiness, confidence, goals, learning preferences, prior experience, and course-specific knowledge.

Instructors receive actionable dashboards

Results are organized into cohort-level insights, student profiles, KPI reports, and instructional recommendations.

Student Assessment Framework

CohortView combines standardized readiness questions with instructor-customized course content questions.

Academic Readiness

  • Reading confidence
  • Writing confidence
  • Technology skills
  • Study habits
  • Time management

Learning Preferences

  • Video-based learning
  • Reading-based learning
  • Interactive activities
  • Hands-on practice
  • Independent or collaborative work

Goals & Experience

  • Educational goals
  • Career aspirations
  • Workforce interests
  • Prior coursework
  • Industry experience

Course-Specific Knowledge

Instructors can edit targeted content questions to assess what students already know before the course begins.

Consistent Reporting

The larger assessment framework stays consistent, allowing instructors and institutions to compare readiness trends across sections, programs, or cohorts.

How It Works

CohortView connects formative assessment, student profiles, instructor dashboards, and data-informed teaching.

1. Assess

Students complete a readiness and course knowledge assessment before instruction begins.

2. Analyze

CohortView organizes responses into individual profiles and cohort-level instructional insights.

3. Act

Faculty use dashboards and recommendations to adapt instruction, support students, and improve planning.

Instructional Recommendations

CohortView does more than report data. It helps instructors translate data into instructional action.

Example Recommendation

Students scored below 50% on foundational course vocabulary. Consider adding a glossary activity, guided notes, or a short vocabulary review before Module 1.

Example Recommendation

Most students selected visual or video-based learning preferences. Consider adding diagrams, short videos, infographics, or visual examples to early lessons.
