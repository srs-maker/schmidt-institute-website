What Is CohortView™?

Instead of guessing what students need, CohortView provides actionable instructional intelligence before instruction begins.

Student Assessment

  • Content readiness
  • Learning preferences
  • Educational goals
  • Career interests
  • Prior experience
  • Confidence levels
  • Areas of strength
  • Areas needing support

Instructor Intelligence

  • Cohort readiness reports
  • Instructional recommendations
  • KPI dashboards
  • Learning profile summaries
  • Risk indicators
  • Student support planning
  • Program-level analytics
  • Data-informed teaching insights

How It Works

1. Student Assessment

Students complete a customizable formative assessment aligned to instructor goals.

2. Data Analysis

Responses are analyzed across instructional, demographic, and learning dimensions.

3. Instructor Dashboard

Faculty receive actionable dashboards highlighting strengths, risks, and opportunities.
