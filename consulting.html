Signature Engagements

Engagements can be customized based on institutional size, goals, timeline, and implementation needs.

Instructional Innovation Starter

A focused engagement designed to assess current instructional practices and identify opportunities for improvement.

Best for: departments, small programs, pilot projects, and early planning.

Student Success Accelerator

A comprehensive engagement focused on improving student readiness, persistence, faculty support, and instructional decision making.

Best for: colleges, districts, workforce programs, and grant-funded initiatives.

CohortView™ Pilot Partnership

An implementation partnership for institutions interested in testing and refining instructional intelligence strategies.

Best for: early adopters, innovation teams, student success leaders, and faculty development programs.
